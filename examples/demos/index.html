HOW DEMOS PLAY

A demo plays its rendered MP4 when one has been built, otherwise the live engine plays the project scene by scene in your browser. Every film starts at 0:00.

BUILD THE VIDEOS

node scripts/build-demos.cjs --video --ffmpeg <path> renders any missing MP4s at 1280×720. The editable project files are always included.
